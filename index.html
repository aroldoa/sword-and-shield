Common Questions

Everything you need to know about Sword & Shield membership

Is my membership tax-deductible?

Yes! Cornerstone Christian Schools is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit. You'll receive a receipt for tax purposes.

Can I designate my donation to a specific program?

While Sword & Shield supports all programs holistically, you can express preferences. Contact us to discuss.

What's the difference between family membership and business sponsorship?

Family memberships are individual/family contributions. Business sponsorships include marketing benefits and brand visibility.

When does my membership expire?

All memberships run on the academic year (August–July). You'll receive a renewal reminder.

Can I pay in installments?

Yes! We offer quarterly payment plans. Contact us to arrange.
